How we work.

Four steps, one in-house team. You approve the story before a single frame is produced.

  1. Discovery & Strategy

    We align on the goal, audience, and metric. You leave with a clear concept and a video mapped to a real business outcome.

  2. Script & Storyboard

    We write the script and storyboard every scene, so you can see and approve the full story before a single frame is produced.

  3. Production & Motion

    Live-action, screen capture, motion design, and animation come together, accurate to your product and on brand throughout.

  4. Post & Delivery

    Editing, sound, and color finish the piece. You receive the master plus channel-ready cutdowns for web, social, and sales.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does video production cost?

It depends on scope, format, and volume, but most SaaS and B2B projects start at around $5,000 for a single explainer or product demo and scale up for full launch sets with multiple cutdowns. Book a call and we’ll give you a transparent estimate for your specific goal.

How much does an explainer or product demo video cost?

A focused explainer video or product demo video typically sits at the entry end of our range, while pricing scales with length, animation complexity, and the number of channel versions you need. We’ll quote a fixed range once we understand the brief.

How long does a video production project take?

Most projects ship in weeks, not months. Timeline depends on scope and review speed, but our in-house process is built to move fast, and product launch work can be fast-tracked when you have a date to hit.

What does the video production process look like?

Four stages: discovery and strategy, script and storyboard, production and motion, then post and delivery. You approve the concept and storyboard before production begins, so there are no expensive surprises.

What deliverables do I receive?

A polished master video plus channel-ready cutdowns and aspect ratios for web, social, and sales. We deliver in the formats your team actually uses, ready to publish.

Can you create product videos that match our actual UI?

Yes, this is our advantage. Because we design digital products ourselves, your demo and explainer videos reflect your real interface and UX, not a faked mockup. The result is accurate, credible, and genuinely clear.

Do you offer B2B and SaaS video production specifically?

Yes. SaaS and B2B video production is our focus. We understand technical products, longer sales cycles, and the metrics that matter, from adoption and conversion to pipeline and fundraising.

How does video actually drive business results?

Strong video shortens the time it takes a buyer to understand your product, builds trust, and moves them to act. We design each piece around a specific outcome: more trials, faster deals, higher adoption, or a successful raise.

Do you produce investor and product launch videos?

Yes. We produce pitch and investor films, fundraising assets, and full launch video sets for startups and technology companies preparing for a key moment.

Why choose a video production agency over in-house or a freelancer?

You get strategy, production, and post under one accountable team, with product fluency a generalist studio or solo freelancer can’t match. That means less management on your side and video that’s built to perform, not just to look good.
